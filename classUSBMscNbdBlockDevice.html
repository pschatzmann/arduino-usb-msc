Detailed Description

USBMscBlockDevice on top of an nbd::NBDClient (512-byte blocks).

The client must be connected before the device is used and must outlive it. The adapter does not connect, reconnect or disconnect: if the connection drops, every call fails until you call connect() on the client again.

WiFiClient wifi;
nbd::NBDClient nbd_client(wifi);
USBMscNbdBlockDevice remote(nbd_client);
USBMscAutoFS<USBMscExt4FS> fs;
nbd_client.connect("192.168.1.10", nbd::NBD_DEFAULT_PORT, "disk");
fs.begin(remote);
