Detailed Description

USBMscBlockDevice backed by an SdFat SD card (SPI or SDIO).

Gives USBDeviceMSC raw sector access to the whole card, so the PC sees it as a USB stick whatever filesystem it holds (FAT, exFAT, ext2/3/4). The board never interprets the filesystem. Initialise the card with sd.cardBegin() (not sd.begin()) so cards without a FAT filesystem work too.

SdFat sd;
sd.cardBegin(SdSpiConfig(CS_PIN, SHARED_SPI));
USBMscSdCard disk(sd.card());
USBDeviceMSC msc;
msc.begin(disk);
